Lantz Home Services: 4 quick fixes that could bring in more calls

  1. No clear call-to-action on first screenRight here at the top, the first thing a visitor sees, there's no clear button like 'Get a free quote' or 'Call now'. Most people decide in about five seconds, so a lot of them just leave without contacting you.
  2. Phone number isn't tap-to-call on mobileOn a phone, most of your customers want to call you straight away. Right now there's no tap-to-call button, so they have to copy the number by hand, and many won't bother.
  3. No easy way to contact youI couldn't find a quick contact form or a clickable phone number on the home page. Every extra click you ask for loses some of the people who were ready to hire you.
  4. No reviews or testimonials shownI didn't see any customer reviews on the site. For local services, reviews are the number one thing that builds trust. Showing a few real ones near the top can lift enquiries a lot.
